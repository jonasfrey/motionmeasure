Detection settings

Analysis copies are cached at up to 256 pixels wide and 2 samples/sec by default. GPU decoding and scaling are used when available, with automatic CPU fallback. Area thresholds adjust automatically with analysis width. An explicit samples/sec rate preserves the minimum positive-motion time and warm-up time implied by the Nth-frame settings, rounded up to whole samples. Lower rates can miss brief motion. The background model continues across adjoining files and resets at known recording gaps. Best for stationary cameras and constant-frame-rate footage. Playback uses the original video resolution and audio; clean exports use original footage with audio, at up to 1080p / 30 fps. Successful reanalysis resets the selection; previous exports are kept.
